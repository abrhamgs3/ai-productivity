\section{Data}

We assemble a country-year panel from public macroeconomic and technology-related datasets and harmonize all inputs to a common country-year structure. Core source systems include the World Development Indicators (WDI), Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI), and Penn World Table (PWT) \cite{worldbankWDI2024,kaufmannKraayMastruzzi2010,feenstraInklaarTimmer2015}. The cleaned analysis file is stored at \texttt{data/processed/panel\_clean.csv}. Core variables include a proxy for AI adoption, log TFP, log GDP per capita, and log human capital.

Data quality checks are automated and exported to \texttt{tables/data\_validation\_report.json}. After removing World Bank regional and income-group aggregates and non-sovereign territories from the raw source files, the cleaned panel covers 193 sovereign countries over 15 years (2,895 country-year observations) before complete-case filtering. Estimation samples are materially smaller because key outcomes and regressors are missing for many country-year observations.

For transparency, coverage differs by model. The baseline TFP specification includes a subset of countries with jointly observed TFP, AI, and human capital variables, while growth and lagged models include partially different subsets due to transformation requirements. This model-dependent sample composition is a core feature of the data and is reported explicitly in output tables.

This missingness pattern has two implications. First, model-by-model sample composition differs, so coefficient comparisons should consider potential composition effects. Second, external validity is stronger for countries with sustained data coverage: only 98 of the 193 countries report AI data in at least one year, and Section~6 shows that these countries differ systematically from the rest of the panel.

Measurement quality is also uneven across variables. AI proxies may capture broader digital intensity in addition to AI-specific deployment, and productivity statistics can vary in comparability across statistical systems \cite{worldbank2021wdr}. In extraction scripts, AI-proxy construction combines patent and digital-infrastructure indicators and, where applicable, standardized composite-index construction using principal-component logic \cite{worldbankWDI2024,jolliffeCadima2016}. These limitations do not invalidate analysis, but they motivate conservative interpretation and careful robustness design.
