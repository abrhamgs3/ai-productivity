# Root Paper Positioning (Policy-Facing Evidence Paper)

Working manuscript: `paper/main.tex`

## Core Question
- Is AI adoption associated with higher productivity and growth in a cross-country panel under standard applied robustness checks?

## Intended Contribution
- Deliver a clear, reproducible baseline evidence paper for policy and broad applied-economics audiences.
- Emphasize interpretable effect sizes and stable directional findings under practical robustness checks.

## Scope Boundaries
- Keep methods compact: FE/TWFE, trimming, lag sensitivity, clustering alternatives, placebo outcome.
- Avoid full diagnostics architecture in main narrative (event-study lead tests, BH family correction, extended influence diagnostics) as central novelty claims.

## Audience and Venue Fit
- Applied policy journals, development policy outlets, macro policy discussions.
- Readers value clarity, transparent workflow, and practical interpretation over maximal methodological depth.

## Main Claim Style
- "Robust conditional association" language only.
- No structural causal claim.

## Distinction from Advanced Manuscript
- Root paper = concise policy-facing baseline and moderate robustness portfolio.
- Advanced manuscript = technical diagnostics paper with expanded inferential stress tests and identification matrix.
